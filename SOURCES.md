## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin hold first three-way summit (Asia / diplomacy)
- Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met on Thursday at Kylin
  Villa in Shenzhen — the first-ever three-way meeting of the American,
  Chinese and Russian leaders — for about an hour and a half, after Trump
  met Putin for ~40 minutes bilaterally and Xi separately.
- The three issued a joint declaration: reform the UN; increase production
  and supply of refined petroleum products; state consent and official
  immunity matter and the ICC must not abuse its jurisdiction; and "a
  nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought", with a dialogue on
  reducing nuclear risks. It offered few specifics and no deal on Ukraine
  or Iran.

## 2. UN court: Myanmar committed genocide (law / human rights)
- The International Court of Justice ruled on Thursday in The Hague that
  Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 clearance
  operations — the first time the court has held a state responsible for
  genocide. The court found ~10,000 Rohingya were violently killed and
  ordered Myanmar to make reparations.
- The case was brought by The Gambia in 2019; the ruling was by nine votes
  to six, read out by presiding Judge Yuji Iwasawa over nearly three hours.
  The ruling is final and cannot be appealed, though the court has limited
  means to enforce it.

## 5. Ukraine hits Kerch Bridge a third time (war)
- Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to Crimea for the third
  time in four days, overnight on 19 Nov: Neptune missiles hit the rail
  section near the arch and railway tracks, and train traffic was suspended
  (resuming Thursday afternoon). Russia's transport ministry reported no
  casualties.
- The road section had already been shut since a strike on 17 Nov dropped
  a span into the Kerch Strait; the bridge is a key supply route for
  Russian forces in Crimea.

## 6. Cyclone Akvan: 80,000 evacuated in Odisha (weather / Asia)
- Severe cyclonic storm Akvan in the Bay of Bengal drove mass evacuations
  in Odisha on Thursday: more than 80,000 people were moved to safety
  (the count rising through the day toward a 200,000 target), 74 rescue
  teams were deployed, and schools were shut in six coastal districts.
- The India Meteorological Department said Akvan would cross the north
  Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha coasts between Narsapur and Puri, near
  Gopalpur, by Friday noon, with sustained winds of 90–100 km/h gusting to
  110 km/h.
